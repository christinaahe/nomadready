## What this data can't tell you

* **Neighborhood Safety & Local Security**: While the data provides country-level travel advisories, it cannot verify the localized safety of specific neighborhoods like Palermo, Copacabana, or Cuauhtémoc.
* **Visas & Immigration**: There is no data regarding visa requirements, digital nomad visas, or stay limits for US citizens in Chile, Argentina, Mexico, or Brazil.
* **Atmosphere and Nightlife**: The findings do not cover social scenes, nightlife, culinary quality, or local transportation options.
* **Pricing Snapshot Limitations**: Prices are based on a September snapshot's nightly rate x nights, not live quotes, and do not include weekly or monthly discounts.
* **Availability Changes**: Property availability may have changed since the data snapshot was taken.
* **Internet Speed Measurements**: Speeds are measured around the listing's geographical location, not the individual unit's Wi-Fi.
