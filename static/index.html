NomadReady

Find where to work remotely for a week or more, and which neighbourhood to stay in.

For remote workers planning a stay abroad. Tell NomadReady when you're going, for how long, your budget and the hours you need to be online. It researches each city in parallel, compares neighbourhoods on real listing and internet-speed data, and cites every number.

What it checks

  • Airbnb stays open for your dates, and what they cost
  • Measured home-internet speeds around each listing
  • Typical weather for your month
  • Your work hours in local time
  • The U.S. travel advisory level
  • Coworking spaces nearby (Google Maps)

How it works

  1. The Lead reads your request and asks if something key is missing
  2. One Researcher per city works in parallel with the data tools
  3. The Lead spots gaps and sends follow-ups
  4. The Writer drafts; the Verifier checks every number against the data

What it can't tell you

  • Visa rules and how long you can stay
  • Safety, noise or a neighbourhood's feel
  • Live prices or discounts (it uses a snapshot)
  • A specific apartment's Wi-Fi
  • Hotels, leases or sublets
